Architectural works built for tactile permanence.

We approach architecture from the interior outward. Each project stands as an exploration of material gravity, pairing the weight of poured structural clay with the softness of sand-washed finishes and custom joinery.

Project 01 / Austin

The Sandstone Monolith

A low-slung, poured earth pavilion carved into a gentle hillside. Designed to block the harsh afternoon southern sun while preserving deep shadow play and cool interior stone pathways.

Materials Clay-mix, Travertine

Project 02 / Sedona

The Clay & Canopy Pavilion

An architectural study in earthen clay walls meeting thin, cantilevered timber canopies. Glass boundaries vanish to create a single continuous floor plane running from hearth to outer deck.

Materials Raw Oak, Red Earth Plaster
Scale 2,800 sq.ft

Ochre Hilltop Residence
