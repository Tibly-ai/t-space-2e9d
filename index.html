02 / Purpose

A design practice rooted in physical weight and earthborn textures.

The contemporary world is increasingly virtual and lightweight. We counter this displacement by anchoring our projects in physical presence. By choosing materials that ask to be touched, we cultivate interiors that provide quiet sanctuary.

Our methodology treats interior architectural elements as permanent installations. We focus on monolithic stone kitchen islands, custom lime-plaster wall applications, and untreated timber framing. This intentional weight defines how sunlight interacts with the planes of a home.
